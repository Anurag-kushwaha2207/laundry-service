Damage & Security Deposit Policy

Fair, transparent, and hassle-free protection for both Renter and Owner.

🛡️ 1. How Security Deposit Works

When you rent an outfit, a refundable Security Deposit is held securely until the outfit is safely returned and inspected. Once our laundry team inspects the outfit upon return, the deposit is automatically refunded back to your payment source within 24-48 hours.

📊 2. Condition & Deduction Matrix

Condition / Damage Type Examples Deduction Amount
Normal Wear & Tear Minor dust, light wrinkles, normal usage scent ₹0 (Fully Covered)
Minor Washable Stain Food spill, mud splatter removable via standard dry cleaning ₹100 - ₹200
Major Stain / Alteration Ink, oil stain, unauthorized stitching or safety pin tears 30% of Deposit
Fabric Tear / Burn Iron burn marks, torn embroidery, missing embellishments 50% of Deposit
Total Loss / Unreturned Item lost, stolen, or irreparable structural damage 100% of Deposit

✨ 3. Professional Cleaning Guarantee

Every outfit is professionally dry cleaned and sanitized before delivery to the Renter, and again after return to the Owner. Renters are requested NOT to wash or iron delicate designer wear at home.
